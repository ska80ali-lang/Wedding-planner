The Hidden Financial Trap

Here's what nobody tells you before you start planning

20–30%
on top of the "final" budget

Hidden costs are the rule, not the exception. GST, service charges, overtime across every vendor, vendor meals, generator fuel — they routinely add 20–30% on top of the number you thought was final.

Last
when it should be first

The single biggest lever — the guest list — is usually finalised last, after the venue is already booked. Backwards: the guest count decides venue size, catering, seating and return-gift quantities.

78.65%
of families fund it from savings

Most Indian weddings are paid for out of personal savings. There is very little room in that budget for an avoidable ₹5 lakh mistake — one wrong vendor contract or a missed retainer clause is all it takes.
